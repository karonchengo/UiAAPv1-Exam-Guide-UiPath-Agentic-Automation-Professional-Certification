## What Is the UiPath Agentic Automation Professional Certification?

The UiPath Certified Professional Agentic Automation Professional certification belongs to the UiPath Certified Professional – Agentic Automation Track. It is designed for automation professionals who work with intelligent agents and need to understand how to integrate them into enterprise automation environments.

Unlike traditional automation approaches that primarily depend on predefined rules and workflows, agentic automation introduces autonomous agents that can interpret goals, make decisions, interact with tools, and coordinate with other agents, robots, and human users.

The certification focuses on the practical knowledge required to implement these solutions reliably and securely. Certified professionals should be capable of designing agent-driven workflows, integrating agents with enterprise platforms, applying governance policies, and monitoring agent performance.

The credential is particularly relevant as organizations increasingly combine traditional robotic process automation (RPA), generative AI, autonomous agents, and human oversight to create more adaptable business processes.

## Who Should Take the UiPath Agentic Automation Professional Exam?

This certification is intended for automation professionals who design, implement, and manage enterprise agentic automation solutions.

The primary target audience includes:
- **Automation Developers:** Professionals responsible for creating agents, automation workflows, and integrations.
- **Solution Architects:** Professionals who design agentic automation architectures and coordinate agents, robots, enterprise applications, and human interactions.
- **Technical Leads:** Individuals responsible for technical implementation, integration, reliability, and governance.
- **Center of Excellence (CoE) Members:** Professionals who establish standards, governance practices, and operational frameworks for enterprise automation.

A minimally qualified candidate should be able to independently design, build, orchestrate, and govern agentic automation solutions using the UiPath platform. This includes integrating agents with enterprise systems and applying appropriate operational and governance practices.
